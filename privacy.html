Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

This is my personal portfolio site. It collects as little as possible. This page explains exactly what that means.

What I collect

The only information I actively collect is what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address and your message. I use it to read your message and reply to you. I do not sell it, share it or add it to any mailing list.

Who processes it

  • Formspree handles the contact form. When you submit it, your name, email and message are sent to Formspree, which forwards them to my inbox and keeps a copy in my account there. Their privacy policy applies to that step.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web host it records standard server request information, which can include your IP address and browser user agent.
  • Google Fonts serves the two typefaces this site uses. Because the font files are requested from Google servers, Google receives your IP address when the page loads. No other third party script runs on this site.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies, and there is no analytics, advertising, fingerprinting or tracking of any kind. Nothing follows you between sites.

What stays on your device

If you switch between the light and dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key theme. It never leaves your device and I cannot read it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

How long I keep messages

I keep contact form messages for as long as the conversation is useful, and delete them after that. If you want your message deleted sooner, email me and I will remove it.

Your choices

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email magistradozach@gmail.com and I will act on it. You can also simply not use the form and email me directly instead.

Changes

If this policy changes I will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about any of this go to magistradozach@gmail.com.
